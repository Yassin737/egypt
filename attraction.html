My favourite places to see in Egypt

The Great Pyramids

The Great Pyramids of Giza, part of the Giza Plateau, are massive tombs built for Egyptian pharaohs during the Old Kingdom period (roughly 2613–2494 BC). The largest and most famous is the Great Pyramid of Khufu, which stood as the tallest man-made structure for over 3,800 years. It's one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World It's one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World and remains mostly intact. The complex also includes the Pyramid of Khafre, a smaller pyramid for Khufu's son, and the Pyramid of Menkaure.

Khan El Khalili

Khan el-Khalili is a bustling bazaar and cultural hub located in the historic center of Cairo, Egypt. It's a maze of narrow streets, shops, and traditional buildings, offering a vibrant and sensory experience for visitors. The market is famous for its diverse goods, including traditional crafts, souvenirs, spices, perfumes, and more. Khan el-Khalili is also known for its traditional coffeehouses where you can enjoy a drink and experience the local culture.

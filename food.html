My favourite food in Egypt

Koshari

Koshari is a beloved Egyptian dish that combines pasta, rice, and lentils, topped with a garlicky tomato sauce, crispy fried onions, and other optional toppings like fried vermicelli and chickpeas. It's considered Egypt's national dish and is a popular street food. Koshari is a flavorful and filling dish, often enjoyed as a fast food option.

Ful w Taameya

"Ful w Taameya" (also spelled "Ful wa Ta'ameya" or "Ful wa Ta'ameya") is a popular Egyptian street food dish consisting of two main components: Ful (mashed fava beans) and Ta'ameya (Egyptian falafel). It's a staple in Egyptian cuisine, especially as a breakfast or snack.
